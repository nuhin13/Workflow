# D-001 — Deferred support-incidence definition

- Between: `NFR-ADOPTION-02` ↔ `Q-006`
- Detected: 2026-07-29 by Codex during `/srs-authoring`
- Severity: medium (the KPI cannot be implemented or verified while undefined)
- Status: accepted

## The mismatch

`NFR-ADOPTION-02` requires qualifying support incidence to be no more than 5%
of the 100-workshop target cohort by month 4. `Q-006` asks what event makes a
workshop count as “requiring support.” The project owner approved SRS v1 while
explicitly deferring that operational definition.

## Impact

The rest of SRS v1 can be traced and planned. `NFR-ADOPTION-02` cannot be
implemented, instrumented, or verified without inventing which support events
count. Work that claims to satisfy that NFR is frozen.

## Resolution options

1. Adopt the current recommendation: count a workshop once when it needs human
   intervention after onboarding to complete a core workflow; exclude planned
   research and product-wide outage notices.
2. Approve a different measurable event definition and exclusions.
3. Accept the definition gap temporarily and freeze `NFR-ADOPTION-02`.
4. Remove or defer the KPI through a new approved business-source revision.

## Decision

Option 3 was selected by the project owner while approving SRS v1 on
2026-07-29. Resolve Q-006 through an approved SRS amendment before pilot
instrumentation or any task claims `NFR-ADOPTION-02`. SRS approval commit:
`4c246a1`.
